## Claude · 17:17

I'll check what the project's own logs and docs say about reuse factor before answering, so I'm not guessing.

Short answer: it's partly that, but the bigger reason is structural, not the DSP count. The project logs record three separate things going on.

**1. The DSP half of it (true, but only for the binary matmuls).** In hls4ml's default `Latency` strategy, ReuseFactor doesn't fold the datapath. It sets a pipeline II target and a cap on how many `mul` operations Vitis may allocate, so its whole effect is "share the multipliers." With ±1 weights Vitis never instantiates a multiplier in the first place (a 1-signed-digit weight is a wire or a negation), so there is nothing for RF to share. The research log has this from the Sun hls4ml tutorial, slides 38 to 40: "when the number of digits is ≤ 2 ... reuse_factor will not be useful for those operations." And the recovered per-module reports confirmed all 13 binary `einsum_dense` layers were 0 DSP at RF=1, so RF would have had zero multipliers to act on. See [research-log.md:1352-1394](.claude/memory/archive/research-log.md#L1352-L1394).

**2. The real blocker: the whole design is fully unrolled by construction.** hls4ml's io_parallel graph puts a bare `#pragma HLS PIPELINE` on the top-level `myproject`. Vitis fully unrolls every loop inside a pipelined region, regardless of any RF or PF on the inner loops. That was proven the hard way on 2026-08-07: the PF=8 retry produced bit-identical unroll instruction counts to the baseline (21,295,573 both ways), so neither knob ever acted. On top of that, `nnet_einsum.h` unrolls unconditionally anyway. See [experiment-log.md:2307-2320](.claude/memory/archive/experiment-log.md#L2307-L2320).

**3. Why we can't just switch to the strategy where RF does fold.** Real folding (one processing element reused, weights in BRAM) is hls4ml's `Resource` strategy. But hls4ml 1.3.0 asserts that Einsum and EinsumDense are Latency-only, so the attention path can't use it. The hybrid monolith in [convert.py:25-34](publication/code/hgq2/bnhgq2/convert.py#L25-L34) is the workaround: pointwise conv and dense layers run at Resource with a per-layer RF (capped at RF ≤ in_channels), while the weightless attention einsums stay Latency. So "no reuse factor" is really "no reuse factor on the einsum path, and none at all while the top-level PIPELINE pragma stands."

One more point worth holding onto for the paper: even where RF works, the tutorial's rule of thumb is that raising it divides DSPs but leaves LUTs the same or slightly higher from muxing. Our design is LUT-bound with DSP already at zero, so RF was never the lever that would shrink it. The knobs that actually matter here are ParallelizationFactor on the sequence-position loop and stripping the top-level pipeline pragma, and the log notes that the latter turns the design into a folded, deployable-class point rather than the RF=1 max-parallelism reference.
