# NeuraLUT-Assemble: Hardware-aware Assembling of Sub-Neural Networks for Efficient LUT Inference

- **Authors:** Marta Andronic, George A. Constantinides — Dept. of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Imperial College London.
- **Venue / Year:** Published at **two** venues in 2025: **FCCM 2025** (IEEE 33rd Int'l Symp. on Field-Programmable Custom Computing Machines), pp. 208–216; also a workshop paper at **EuroMLSys 2025** (https://euromlsys.eu/pdf/euromlsys25-16-nonACM.pdf).
- **arXiv:** 2504.00592 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00592 (full text read directly this session, all 9 pages)
- **Code:** https://github.com/MartaAndronic/NeuraLUT
- **Topic area:** LUT-native neural networks for FPGAs

## TL;DR
Fixes NeuraLUT's remaining wall: even though a whole sub-network can hide inside one L-LUT, the *boundary* fan-in F is still small and the framework "relies on the reliance on extremely sparse models" (their own §I wording) at the top level. NeuraLUT-Assemble **trees together multiple smaller L-LUTs** into a larger effective input (e.g., four 4-input L-LUTs feeding a combining L-LUT for a 16-input tree), with **skip-connections threaded across the entire assembled tree** (not just within one partition, as in plain NeuraLUT) to preserve gradient flow through the now much-deeper effective network. Headline: closes the LUT-vs-MLP accuracy gap further, with up to **8.42× area-delay-product reduction** vs. state of the art at publication time, and crucially **exposes and resolves a JSC dataset-provenance discrepancy** (see below) that the rest of the family's papers gloss over.

## (a) Mechanism
Explicitly characterizes its own training approach relative to the *whole* prior family (§II.E, verbatim, describing NullaNet/LogicNets/PolyLUT/NeuraLUT collectively): *"while designed for LUT-based netlist inference, the training process relies on traditional neural network models, which are later fully absorbed into LUT functions by complete enumeration."* NeuraLUT-Assemble follows the same pattern at tree scale: **"we train the tree structure from scratch rather than mapping a higher fan-in model onto it"** (their own words, explicit and unambiguous) — i.e. even here, there is no route from an independently pretrained network into the tree; the assembled structure (fixed a priori: layer sizes `w_l`, per-layer fan-ins `F`, bitwidths `β`, sub-network depth/width `L`/`N`, skip-step `S` — Table I) is trained end-to-end via QAT (Brevitas), then each L-LUT converted to Verilog ROM, matching every other member of this family.

## (b) What it explicitly does NOT do
- **Not a checkpoint compiler**, confirmed in the authors' own words (above) — the single most direct primary-source statement found this session on the "training method vs. compiler" axis for the entire family.
- **MLP-only.** Evaluated on "digit classification, jet classification, and network intrusion" (abstract) — no attention/sequence mention.
- Trees still bottom out in small per-L-LUT fan-ins (F=2–6 across configs, Table II); the exponential wall is pushed down to the tree's leaves, not eliminated.

## An important, directly relevant finding: **two non-identical "JSC" datasets circulate in this literature**
NeuraLUT-Assemble is the only paper in this family, read this session, that explicitly names and separates **two different sourcings of "the" jet substructure/jet-tagging dataset**:
- **"JSC CERNBox"** — 986,806 instances (https://cernbox.cern.ch/index.php/s/jvFd5MoWhGs1I5v/download).
- **"JSC OpenML"** — ~830,000 instances (https://www.openml.org/d/42468, the `hls4ml_lhc_jets_hlf` OpenML dataset ID 42468).
Their own stated observation (§IV, verbatim): *"The discrepancy likely arises from different filtering or selection criteria. Experimentally, we observed that models trained on the OpenML dataset achieve higher accuracy, suggesting that its smaller size reflects more stringent filtering or better data curation."* **This means JSC accuracy numbers quoted across different papers in this family are not automatically apples-to-apples** unless the CERNBox-vs-OpenML sourcing is checked — a genuine, citable literature caveat for any comparison table built from this family (including the one below and in our own final report).

## Verdict
**OCCUPIES** the JSC-as-MLP space at (as of this session) the best published latency/area operating points for this exact task. **ORTHOGONAL** to "compile a given trained transformer" — and this is the paper that states most explicitly, in its own words, that the whole family trains its own fixed tree/topology from scratch rather than mapping a higher-fan-in (or externally pretrained) model onto the LUT structure. No attention/sequence support anywhere in the family as of this paper.
