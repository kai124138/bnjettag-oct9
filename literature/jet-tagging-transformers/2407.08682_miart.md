# Jet Tagging with More-Interaction Particle Transformer (MIParT)

- **Authors:** Yifan Wu, Kun Wang, Congqiao Li, Huilin Qu, Jingya Zhu
- **Venue / Year:** Chinese Physics C; 2024 (DOI 10.1088/1674-1137/ad7f3d)
- **arXiv:** 2407.08682 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08682
- **PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.08682
- **Code:** https://github.com/USST-HEP/MIParT
- **Topic area:** Jet tagging & transformers (direct follow-up to Particle Transformer's
  interaction-feature attention)

## TL;DR
A 2024 follow-up to ParT (co-authored by ParT's own Huilin Qu) that replaces ParT's *additive*
interaction bias (`QK^T/√d + U`) with a **More-Interaction Attention (MIA)** that widens the
interaction embedding and — per the extracted formula — derives attention weights directly from
`U` rather than adding it to `QK^T`. Reaches slightly better accuracy/AUC than ParT using only
**~30% of ParT's parameters** (720.9k vs. 2.14M) and matches LorentzNet's accuracy/AUC. No
FPGA/latency study; no constituent-count ablation; still 2–3 orders of magnitude larger than our
18.4k-param target.

## Summary
MIParT keeps ParT's core idea — encode the four pairwise kinematic invariants (`ln Δ, ln k_T,
ln z, ln m²`) into a per-head interaction embedding and use it to bias attention — but increases
the interaction embedding's channel dimension and changes how it enters the attention computation,
allowing the interaction pathway to carry more information without growing the standard
`QK^T`/value pathway's cost proportionally. The result matches or beats LorentzNet's
accuracy/AUC and clearly beats ParT's background rejection at both 50% and 30% signal efficiency
on top tagging, at less than a third of ParT's parameter count.

## Key methods / contributions
- **More-Interaction Attention (MIA)**, contrasted with ParT's P-MHA:
  - ParT: `P-MHA(Q,K,V) = SoftMax(QK^T/√d_k + U) V` (U is an **additive bias** to the logits).
  - MIParT: `MIA(U,V) = SoftMax(U) V` (per WebFetch extraction of arxiv.org/html/2407.08682,
    accessed 2026-07-24) — i.e. **attention weights are derived directly from the widened
    interaction embedding U, not added to a separate QK^T term.** Flag: this may be one branch of
    a larger block that also runs standard attention in parallel/series; the extraction did not
    fully resolve the whole-block wiring — **treat as UNVERIFIED pending a direct PDF/equation
    read** before relying on the "no QK^T at all" reading for a hardware argument.
  - The paper states this design increases interaction-embedding dimensionality "without
    significantly increasing the complexity of the model" (quoted).
- **MIParT-L**: a larger variant (embedding dim 128 vs. 64) for the JetClass-pretrain-then-finetune
  regime, ending up comparable in size to ParT (2.38M vs. 2.14M params).

## Relevance to BNJetTag
MIParT is direct, recent (2024), ParT-lineage evidence that **widening/restructuring the
interaction-feature pathway is worth real accuracy** (+9–25% background rejection over ParT at
matched or 3× fewer parameters) — reinforcing Q1's ablation result rather than complicating it.
But it operates two-plus orders of magnitude above our scale (720.9k vs. our 18.4k), on the
binary top-tagging benchmark rather than our 5-class HLS4ML LHC Jet dataset, with no hardware
study and (per the flagged uncertainty above) a possibly-different attention-mixing mechanism
than ParT's simple additive bias — if MIA turns out to genuinely replace `QK^T` with a
`softmax(U)` pathway, that would be a *more* hardware-relevant mechanism to evaluate than ParT's
own P-MHA (potentially removing one activation×activation attention term, at the cost of the same
interaction-network cost ParT already pays) and would be worth a dedicated follow-up read before
citing further.
